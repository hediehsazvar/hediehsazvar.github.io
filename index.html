Hedieh Sazvar

I'm a fifth-year Ph.D. candidate in Operations Research at Northwestern University, advised by Professor Seyed M.R. Iravani and Professor Sanjay Mehrotra. Graduating 2026 — actively interviewing for Data Scientist, Applied Scientist, and Research Scientist roles.

My research builds optimization and sequential decision-making methods for online matching platforms — settings where a platform allocates limited, perishable capacity to heterogeneous users arriving over time under uncertainty about preferences and follow-through. Applications include healthcare staffing, gig and freelance marketplaces, and volunteer platforms. The methods extend naturally to ad bidding, recommender systems, and dynamic pricing.

I combine mathematical depth — stochastic optimization, integer programming, dynamic programming, reinforcement learning — with hands-on experimentation work from a recent industry internship at Moloco.

Selected publications

Optimal Sequential Job Recommendations with No-Shows. In preparation for Manufacturing & Service Operations Management.
Threshold-optimal recommendation policies under no-shows; strongly polynomial-time exact algorithm and a nested-structure heuristic.

Optimization Models and Reformulations for Job Recommender Systems with Dependent Preferences and No-Shows. In preparation for INFORMS Journal on Computing.
MIP reformulations under multinomial logit, Markov chain choice, and exponomial models, strengthened with valid inequalities.

Rollout-Augmented Lookahead Policies for Dynamic Join Decisions in Fork-Join Queues. In preparation.
Lookahead with simulation-based rollout for sequential information-gathering decisions; benchmarked against threshold heuristics and tabular Q-learning.
